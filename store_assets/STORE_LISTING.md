## Long Description (English)
FitTrack is your ultimate offline gym companion and fitness tracker designed for total privacy and performance.

Key Features:
- 🏋️ **Pre-loaded Exercise Library**: Over 40+ exercises with instructions, plus the ability to add custom exercises.
- 📋 **Workout Plans & Routines**: Create custom routines with target sets and reps assigned to days of the week.
- ⏱️ **Active Workout Logging**: Live duration timer, set checkmarks, +/- weight and reps steppers, and adjustable rest timer with vibration.
- 🔥 **Streak Counter & Monthly Calendar**: Track consecutive workout days and view your monthly consistency at a glance.
- 📊 **Progress & Charts**: Track body weight and measurements with pure Compose Canvas line charts and automatic Personal Record (PR) badges.
- 🥗 **Nutrition & Water Tracker**: Track meals with calorie and macro targets (protein, carbs, fat) and quick-add water logger (+250 ml).
- 🧮 **Fitness Calculators**: Built-in BMI, TDEE/BMR, and 1RM strength calculators.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private**: All data stays on your device with Full JSON Backup & Export.
