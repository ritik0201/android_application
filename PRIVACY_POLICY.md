# Privacy Policy

**FitTrack**
**Effective Date: October 2026**
**Last Updated: October 2026**

FitTrack respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the FitTrack Android application.

FitTrack is designed with a **local-first and privacy-focused approach**. Your fitness data is stored on your device and is not transmitted to FitTrack's servers.

---

## 1. Information You Provide

FitTrack allows you to enter information to help you track your fitness and health-related activities.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

* Profile information
* Workout and exercise records
* Body measurements
* Weight and progress information
* Meal and nutrition records
* Progress photos
* Other information you voluntarily enter into the application

This information is used solely to provide and operate FitTrack's features.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored

FitTrack is designed to store your data **locally on your Android device**.

The application uses local Android storage technologies, including:

* **Room Database**
* **Android DataStore**

Your FitTrack data is not intentionally uploaded to FitTrack-operated servers.

You can therefore use the core functionality of FitTrack without creating an online account or depending on cloud storage.

---

## 3. No Tracking or Advertising

FitTrack does not use your personal fitness information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

The application does not intentionally use:

* Advertising networks
* Advertising IDs
* Analytics SDKs
* Tracking technologies
* Behavioral profiling
* Third-party advertising services

FitTrack does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. No Cloud Synchronization

FitTrack does not provide cloud synchronization for your fitness data.

Your information remains stored locally on your device unless **you explicitly choose to export or share it** using functionality provided by the application or your Android device.

---

## 5. Data Export

FitTrack provides data-export functionality so that you can maintain control over your information.

Depending on the features available in your version of FitTrack, you may be able to export:

**Workout History**
Export workout information in CSV format.

**Application Backup**
Create a JSON backup of supported FitTrack data.

Exported files are handled through your device's standard Android file and sharing functionality.

Once you choose to share or store an exported file outside FitTrack, that file is subject to the privacy and security practices of the service or location where you choose to keep it.

---

## 6. Data Deletion

You remain in control of your locally stored FitTrack information.

You can delete your data using the **Clear All App Data** option available in FitTrack's settings, where supported.

You can also uninstall the FitTrack application from your device.

Please note that Android device-level backup or restoration features may be controlled by Android or your device manufacturer. FitTrack does not independently control such system-level services.

---

## 7. Data Sharing

FitTrack does not intentionally share your stored fitness information with third parties.

Your information is not sold, rented, or provided to advertisers.

If you choose to export or share information from FitTrack yourself, the information may become accessible to the person, application, or service that you choose to share it with.

---

## 8. Data Security

FitTrack is designed to minimize exposure of your personal information by keeping application data stored locally on your device.

However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your Android device and any exported FitTrack files or backups.

We recommend using an appropriate device lock, such as a PIN, password, fingerprint, or other supported security mechanism.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

FitTrack does not knowingly collect children's personal information through remote servers or online services.

Because FitTrack is designed primarily for local data storage, parents or guardians should use appropriate supervision when children use the application.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services

FitTrack's core functionality does not require external servers, cloud services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or third-party APIs.

If future versions introduce online services or third-party integrations, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how those services handle information.

---

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FitTrack's functionality changes or when necessary to reflect applicable legal or privacy requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published through the appropriate FitTrack application or website.

The **Last Updated** date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
