# FitTrack Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026

FitTrack is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the FitTrack Android application.

## 🔒 100% Offline & On-Device Storage
FitTrack is designed as a completely offline, local-first application.
* **No Remote Servers**: FitTrack does not communicate with external servers, cloud services, or third-party APIs.
* **No Cloud Backups**: All your data (including user profile, workouts, body measurements, meal logs, and progress photos) is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage using Room Database and DataStore.
* **No Tracking or Analytics**: FitTrack contains zero analytics SDKs, advertising IDs, or user tracking tools.

## 📁 Data Export & Control
You retain 100% ownership and control over your data:
* **Exporting Data**: You can export your workout history as a CSV file or create a full JSON backup via the Settings screen.
* **Deleting Data**: You can clear all app data at any time using the "Clear All App Data" option in Settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 📞 Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the app listing or developer contact.
